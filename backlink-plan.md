# Backlink plan — the #1 lever for the position-39 rank problem

Your pages are technically clean and your content is fine. What Google is missing is **authority**: other sites vouching for you (referring domains) and people searching your brand. This is the only thing that moves you from page 4. It's off-site and manual, so this is a doing list, not a coding one.

Ordered by effort-to-payoff. Start at the top.

---

## 1. Partner backlinks (easiest — you already have the relationships)
You send these brands paying customers. A link back is a fair, normal ask.
- **Ask every affiliate partner** (Moshy/Eucalyptus, Mosh, Juniper, Knose, Apollo) and every **Commission Factory merchant you join** whether they have a "featured partners", "as seen in", "our affiliates" or press page you can be listed on.
- **Your `/for-business` partners** and any brand you've written up: ask for a link from their site to the relevant Refer Labs page.
- Script: "We drive [X] enquiries/signups to you and feature you on referlabs.com.au. Could you add us to your partners/press page? Happy to reciprocate a mention."
- Target the **money/comparison pages**, not just the homepage (e.g. link to `/juniper`, `/pet-insurance`, `/best-weight-loss-telehealth-australia`).

## 2. SourceBottle + Qwoted (free, ongoing, high-authority links)
The Australian equivalent of HARO. Journalists post requests; you answer as an independent AU comparison expert and earn links from real media outlets.
- Sign up: **SourceBottle** (AU-focused), **Qwoted**, **Featured.com**.
- Set alerts for: weight loss / telehealth, home batteries / solar / energy bills, pet insurance, small-business finance, consumer money/comparison.
- Answer 3-5 a week with a tight, quotable, genuinely useful reply + your name and referlabs.com.au. A handful of media links moves rank more than dozens of directory links.

## 3. A link-worthy data asset (digital PR)
Journalists link to original numbers. You already have the raw material.
- **"Home battery rebate by state 2026"** (you have `/home-battery-rebate-by-state-australia`) — package the state-by-state figures as a shareable table/explainer and pitch it to AU energy/personal-finance journalists.
- Or **"What weight-loss telehealth actually costs in Australia 2026"** — a cost breakdown across pathways.
- Pitch angle: "Independent, up-to-date AU data on [topic], free to cite." One good pickup = several strong links.

## 4. Niche directories & relevant roundups (quick citations)
- Reputable AU business directories and comparison-site listings (skip spammy link farms).
- Get listed in "best AU comparison sites / best affiliate sites" style roundups.
- Find where competitors are linked (GSC > Links, or a free Ahrefs/Semrush backlink check on a competitor) and pursue the same sources.

## 5. Guest posts / expert contributions
- Offer a genuinely useful piece to AU blogs in health, personal finance, or home/energy in exchange for an author link.
- Angle: independent, no-paid-rankings comparison expert.

## 6. Convert unlinked brand mentions
- Search for "Refer Labs" mentions that don't link, and ask for the link. As your brand search grows (via Instagram), this pipeline grows too.

---

## Brand search (the quiet twin of backlinks)
Google treats "people searching *Refer Labs*" as a trust signal. Your Instagram is perfect for this:
- Put the brand name in every post/bio so people Google it.
- Encourage "search Refer Labs" over raw links occasionally.

## What to measure (free, forever)
- **GSC > Links** — referring domains over time (the number that must go up).
- **GSC > Performance** — impressions and average position trending up as authority builds.
- Don't expect movement for weeks. Backlinks compound; they don't spike.

## The one rule
Quality over quantity. Five links from real AU media/partners beat 200 directory links. Never buy links or use PBNs; Google penalises it and it's not worth the risk to a site whose whole moat is trust.
