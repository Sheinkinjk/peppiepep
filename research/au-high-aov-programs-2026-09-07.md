# Australian high-AOV affiliate programs: verification

**Date:** 7 September 2026
**Filters applied:** Australian program (accepts AU publishers, AUD, services AU customers) **and** either AOV ≥ A$150 or expected commission ≥ A$40.
**Read-only.** No website files edited, nothing committed, nothing deployed.

## The single most important finding

**My Muscle Chef pays 0% commission on any order that includes a coupon code**, and 0% on repeat subscription orders. Verbatim from its Commission Factory page:

> "0% commission will be issued to any order that includes a referral coupon code
> 0% commission will be issued on any order that includes a corporate code
> 0% commission will be issued on repeat subscription orders (ie subscription customers will only be eligible for commission on their first subscription order)"

Two of the three highest-value Australian meal programs also bar coupon-site placement outright (Lite n' Easy: "except PPC and coupon sites"). For a site whose thesis is that discount codes drive conversion, this category actively penalises the mechanic.

## Programs that pay on FIRST ORDER ONLY

These need different page treatment: the lifetime value of a referral is a single order, so a subscription-framing page ("cheapest ongoing meal plan") monetises far worse than a trial-framing page ("what it costs to start").

| Brand | Exact wording |
|---|---|
| **My Muscle Chef** | "0% commission will be issued on repeat subscription orders (ie subscription customers will only be eligible for commission on their first subscription order)" |
| **Chefgood** | "Commission Rate : 10% New Customer 0% Return Customer" |
| **Lite n' Easy** | Tiers are stated as "commission on **new orders**". Repeat treatment not stated. **UNKNOWN, must be confirmed before building.** |

Reduced-rate-on-repeat (not zero, but a steep drop):

| Brand | Wording |
|---|---|
| **Yes Chef Meals** | "24% for new customers, 4% for existing customers" |
| **My Muscle Chef** | "6% commission on sales for all new customers (3% commission for returning users)" |
| **Chief Nutrition** | "8% commission on new customer sales, 2% commission on return customer sales" (excluded on AOV, listed here for the pattern) |

**The pattern across the whole Australian meal-delivery category: the headline rate is a new-customer rate, and repeat subscription revenue pays little or nothing.** Any page built on "best ongoing meal subscription" is monetising the part of the customer lifecycle these programs do not pay for.

## Australian regulatory constraints on how these can be promoted

Flagged for legal confirmation rather than asserted. I did not verify the statutory detail in this pass, and the site's existing TGA discipline should govern.

- **CGMs and at-home pathology devices are therapeutic goods.** Advertising them to consumers falls under the Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code. The site's existing rule stands: describe the service, never the medicine, and do not make claims about diagnosing or preventing serious disease.
- **Direct-to-consumer genetic and pathology testing is restricted in Australia.** Predictive genetic testing for serious disease is generally required to go through a health practitioner. CircleDNA and i-screen pages must not imply a consumer can self-diagnose. **Confirm the current TGA position on DTC IVDs before building either page.**
- **Meal delivery marketed for weight loss** attracts both the Advertising Code and ACL. Avoid outcome claims entirely.
- **Technogym and Gym Plus both ban paid search on brand terms.** Gym Plus's wording is absolute: "strictly prohibited from running paid advertising campaigns on any search engine platform".
- **Lite n' Easy bars coupon sites.** If a Refer Labs page is structured as a discount-code page, that program's terms would exclude it.

## Method limits

- **Commission Factory's sitemap is incomplete.** It yielded 558 advertisers, but Dineamic (92335) and Eimele (79475) both have programs and neither appears in it. Absence from the directory is not evidence of absence.
- **CF's `?search=` parameter is ignored** and returns page 1 for every query. Enumeration had to come from `sitemap.xml`.
- **`dashboard.commissionfactory.com/Affiliate/Register/<id>/` returns 200 for a fake ID (999999)**, so it is a generic form and is not evidence a given advertiser exists. Only `/advertiser-directory/<slug>/<id>` is probative; it 404s on fake IDs. No Register URL is cited above as proof.
- **UpPromote's marketplace echoes any search term back**, including nonsense, so it was excluded. Impact's marketplace and Refersion's require a login.
- **Lite n' Easy is the weakest row in the table.** Its terms come from a third-party listing, there is no CF directory page under any slug I tried with ID 46063, and its own site carries no network tag. Confirm directly before acting on the A$40 figure.
- AOV for Chefgood, Yes Chef, Dineamic, Eimele and i-screen is genuinely unpublished. Marked UNKNOWN rather than estimated, so those rows cannot be confirmed against Filter 2.
