Run this list once a month in **ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google (AI Overviews)**. For each prompt, note whether Refer Labs is **mentioned/cited** (Y/N) and, if yes, which engine. Track the Y count over time — moving from ~0 to *any* mentions on these non-branded prompts is the signal that your authority is working.

**Baseline (11 Aug 2026, from Searchable):** organic non-branded mention rate ≈ **0%**. Only "Refer Labs"-named prompts hit ~100% (vanity, ignore). Goal: get real mentions on the prompts below.

1. **Answer-first content** — the target question as an H2, then a tight, quotable answer (the treatment applied to /moshy-vs-juniper and /best-ai-sales-tools). Do this on the pages that answer the priority prompts.
2. **Authority** — backlinks + brand search (see backlink-plan.md). AI engines cite sources they already trust; this is the same lever as Google rank.
3. Re-run monthly. Expect movement in months, not weeks.
