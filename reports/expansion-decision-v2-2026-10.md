## 1. Part A verdict: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED

Most transactions cannot have come through a visit: about 69 a month arrive against 75 to 100 Google clicks a month to every page linking Moshy, Mosh or Juniper, an implied 49% to 92% visit-to-sale rate, far above the 10% plausibility line. Bounded with stated assumptions, **57% to 97% of transactions had no referring visit.** But the brief's premise that code-query search volume drives this is **not supported**: Moshy and Mosh code searches reaching the site total a few hundred a month, which cannot produce 69 sales. The code earns where it is **displayed**, on high-impression comparison and review pages (about 19,200 impressions in 90 days of pages whose snippet shows the code, mostly on non-code queries) and in AI answers. A third explanation, redemptions Refer Labs did not cause, cannot be excluded until Moshy confirms when REFERRAL120 was first issued. Two consequences run through everything below: **only a code unique to Refer Labs and credited on redemption earns without a click**, and code-query volume is a weak proxy for where it will earn.
