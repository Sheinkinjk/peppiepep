## Three facts that shape every option

1. **Affiliate alone cannot reach millions at our traffic.** In 90 days, the longevity and sleep pages earned 37 of the site's 639 clicks. Multi-million revenue means owning the product or the customer: distribution, a sponsored TGA listing, or our own brand. That needs paid acquisition, not just search traffic.
2. **US longevity marketing is mostly illegal here.**
   - Listed medicines have no permitted "anti-ageing" indication, and the TGA's 12 Aug 2026 NMN guidance names "anti-aging" as a breach.
   - Food law bans disease claims.
   - An Australian product sells on sleep, muscle, energy or hydration, never lifespan.
3. **The economics are thin at the bottom line.** Listed DTC health peers net 5 to 7% (Hims 5.5%, Thorne 6.9%). A$1M net needs roughly A$15M to A$18M revenue, and the money is made at scale or at exit. Benchmark: Hims paid US$968.5M for Eucalyptus.

## The decision that comes before any of this

If Refer Labs sells or sponsors a product, every page that mentions it becomes that product's advertising under the TGA Code, and disclosure alone may not fix a comparison that includes it (ACCC comparator guidance). Owning a product means:
- a **separate brand and company**
- the product kept **out of Refer Labs' ranked comparisons**
- **TGA-literate legal sign-off** before launch

Refer Labs' value then becomes the audience, content and quizzes that feed it, not neutral placement.
