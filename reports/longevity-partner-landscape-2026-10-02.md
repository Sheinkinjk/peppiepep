## 0. The answer in one screen

1. **The scale gap decides which deals are worth chasing.** Over 90 days (3 Jul to 30 Sep 2026, Search Console page dimension) the longevity and sleep pages earned **37 clicks from 3,967 impressions**. The whole site earned 639. The best longevity page, `/longevity/diagnostics/everlab-vs-prenuvo-vs-i-screen-australia`, earned 16. At that traffic no affiliate deal in this category, however good, produces more than a few thousand dollars a year. Affiliate is still worth doing because it costs an email, but **"multi-million" in longevity can only come from a structure where Refer Labs owns the customer or the product**: distribution, licensing or a sponsored ARTG listing. Those revenues are driven by the distributor's own acquisition spend, not by the site's organic traffic.

2. **The white space is real and specific.** Strong brands with no Australian route today: **Timeline (Mitopure)**, **Viome's subscriptions**, **Superpower**, **Create**, **Plunge**, **HigherDOSE**, **Function Health**, **Levels**, **Tally Health**. Strong brands with a thin, direct-only Australian presence: **Eight Sleep**, **LYMA**, **Lumen**, **Withings**, **Momentous**.

3. **One regulatory finding moves Timeline to the top.** Urolithin A, Mitopure's only active, has a TGA **compositional guideline as a permitted ingredient for listed medicines** (synthetic, HPLC purity not less than 98.5%) ([TGA](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/resources/compositional-guidelines/urolithin); the effective date of 1 October 2025 comes from TGA's search summary and was not shown on the rendered page). The ARTG holds **no Timeline or Mitopure listing**. Timeline's own FAQ says it "currently ships to the United States, Canada, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, France, and Germany", and its footer publishes an international wholesale inbox. A Nestlé- and L'Oréal-backed brand needs an Australian sponsor to enter, and the ingredient pathway is open.

4. **Two brands have told the market they want Australia.** LYMA's own results release (29 Jan 2026) names "Australia and the UAE" as key territories to establish next. Superpower's founder is Australian and its Series A was raised partly to "explore international markets". Neither has a local partner.

5. **The biggest structural conflict is ours, not theirs.** If Refer Labs distributes or sponsors a product, every page that mentions it becomes the sponsor's own advertising under the Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code (no editorial reading is available), and a comparison that includes it needs the commercial relationship stated beside it, on the ACCC's comparator guidance that disclosure "may not be sufficient" to cure paid preference. A distribution business has to run under its own brand and entity, with the comparison site disclosing the relationship everywhere the product appears and never placing it in a ranked list as a neutral entry. That is solvable, but it has to be designed in before the first conversation, not after.

## 3. Top partnership pitches

Eight, ordered by expected value against effort. Contacts are only those printed on the brand's own site, its form, or a LinkedIn title; no email has been inferred.

**Revenue figures in this section are scenarios, not forecasts.** Every input that is an assumption is labelled. The traffic inputs are the measured Search Console figures above.

### Pitch 1. Timeline (Mitopure): Australian market entry as distributor and ARTG sponsor

- **What Refer Labs offers:** an Australian entity to act as importer and distributor, an ARTG listing obtained through a contract regulatory sponsor (Refer Labs is not set up to hold sponsor obligations itself: GMP evidence, adverse-event reporting, recalls, advertising compliance), Australian launch content, and the dated-fact comparison authority the site already has on supplements (`/longevity/supplements/longevity-supplements-evidence-review`).
- **Deal structure:** exclusive Australian distribution for 2 to 3 years with minimum volumes, Refer Labs buying stock at a wholesale price and selling DTC on a subscription under a separate trading name. Lighter alternative: Timeline ships to Australia itself once listed, and Refer Labs earns a revenue share on Australian sales for running sponsorship and launch.
- **Contact:** international@timeline.com (footer, "International Wholesale"); provider portal pro.timeline.com. LinkedIn: international or business-development lead at Amazentis / Timeline.
- **Before the email:** a regulatory consultant's written view on listing a urolithin A product (indications available, label claims, the cost and time to list), and confirmation that no Australian sponsor holds a Mitopure listing under another name.
- **Revenue scenario:** at 500 active Australian subscribers paying parity with the US monthly plan (assumption: about US$69 to US$89 a month), gross revenue is roughly US$415k to US$535k a year; at 2,000 subscribers, US$1.7M to US$2.1M. Distributor gross margin is unknown and would be set by the wholesale price. Acquisition cost is the distributor's, and it is the real constraint: the site's organic traffic alone would not fill 500 subscriptions.
- **Odds:** medium. Timeline wants new markets and publishes the door; it may prefer a pharmacy-chain or Nestlé Health Science route over a small publisher.

### Pitch 2. Everlab: publisher partnership with a per-member fee

- **What Refer Labs offers:** the only third-party page ranking for Everlab-branded queries ("everlab alternatives" position 10.6, "one mri vs everlab" 10.3, "everlab review" 2.5), the whole-body MRI cost page (1,632 impressions at position 42), and a code placement on every diagnostics page.
- **Deal structure:** a flat fee per new paying member, credited on a Refer Labs code (the i-screen model), with the code's full terms printed beside it (Ahpra s133(1)(b); no testimonials, s133(1)(c)). Ask that the fee be set per tier.
- **Contact:** the form at everlab.com.au/become-a-partner (select "health brand" or "professional services"; 1-25 referrals a month is the accurate band), support@everlab.com.au. Careers lists a Partnerships function and a VP Marketing hire.
- **Revenue scenario:** the head-to-head page sent 16 clicks in 90 days. At an assumed 3% of clicks becoming members and an assumed A$100 to A$300 fee, that is under A$1,000 a year at today's rank. Moving the MRI cost page to page one is the lever; the deal is cheap to set up and pays in proportion.
- **Odds:** high; Everlab built the form for exactly this, and it is raising marketing spend.

### Pitch 3. LYMA: Australian launch partner and clinic channel

- **What Refer Labs offers:** an Australian market-entry partner for the territory LYMA has named, launch content on `/health-and-beauty` and longevity pages, and help through ARTG device inclusion via a contract sponsor (a precondition to any therapeutic claim here).
- **Deal structure:** first ask whether LYMA Professional's reported 20% per device extends to a publisher (one A$4,080 sale is about A$816). Bigger version: exclusive Australian distribution for the device and clinic channel, with Refer Labs signing clinics as resellers.
- **Contact:** lyma.life/pages/lyma-for-business (partner enquiry form); the concierge page in the September report. LinkedIn: international expansion or partnerships lead.
- **Revenue scenario:** affiliate at 20% needs sales the site does not yet produce; a few devices a year at today's traffic. Distribution revenue depends on clinic sign-ups, which is a sales job, not an SEO one.

## 6. Compliance notes that apply to every row

- **TGA:** no claim that a test detects disease or that a product extends life; red-light and laser devices need ARTG inclusion for therapeutic claims (Bon Charge and Therabody hold Class IIa entries; none found for LYMA, CurrentBody or HigherDOSE under their own names); NAD and NMN goods are out.
- **Ahpra s133:** pathology, scans and clinic services are regulated health services; no testimonials, and any discount carries its full terms beside it. Vively, Bloody Good and Viome's creator pages carry testimonials or ratings that must not be reproduced.
- **RANZCR:** whole-body MRI screening of people without symptoms is not endorsed; Prenuvo, Everlab's MRI add-on and HealthScreen inherit that on every page.
- **Claims on vendor sites:** Sunlighten (blood pressure), Eight Sleep (hot flashes), Lumen and Withings (weight-medicine pages) carry claims that would be ours if repeated.
