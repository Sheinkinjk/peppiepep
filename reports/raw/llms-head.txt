# Refer Labs

> Refer Labs is an independent Australian comparison publisher, operating since 2022. It researches and publishes comparisons of health services, insurance, energy and business software for Australian readers, working from providers' own published pricing and terms rather than aggregators. Rankings are never sold and cannot be bought. Some pages carry disclosed affiliate links, at no extra cost to the reader.
