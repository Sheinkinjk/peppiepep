## 1. Verdict on H0

**UNTESTABLE WITH AVAILABLE DATA.** "Only codes convert" is not supported, because plain links were never shown to buyers: across 78 plain-link commercial pages Google delivered about 11 clicks in 90 days (weighted position 30), against 267 clicks at position 15 for the 32 code pages. The one plain-link data set quoted as proof (PartnerStack, 1,236 clicks, 0 revenue) is B2B SaaS, a different purchase, and its click count is contaminated by our own link audits. H1 (codes convert better at matched position and intent) has zero matched pairs and no page-level conversions, so no effect size exists.

What the evidence does show points the operator's way by a different route. About 64 transactions a month arrive against about 244 Google clicks a month, and search results showed REFERRAL120 or REFERAL55 about 19,200 times in 90 days to people who did not click. **Revenue follows the visibility of a code the merchant pays us for, not the click.** That is the real asset, and it is also where the risk now sits (section 2, item 1).

## 3. Kill list

| Category | Single reason |
|---|---|
| Mortgages and consumer credit | Naming or ranking a lender is credit assistance (NCCP Act s8(a); RG 203.61 to 203.62). Without an ACL, a consumer inducement tied to a lender's product is unlawful. The same cause sank the 17 lending pages |
| General and life insurance | Legal only as a bare referral (Corporations Regs reg 7.6.01(1)(e)); at the best published rate found (8% of a A$314 premium) A$200k needs about 7,962 policies a year, 2.5 times the site's entire annual Google clicks |
| Private health insurance | Lawful without an AFSL, but no fund or comparator publishes a per-policy rate, and A$200k at A$200 a policy needs 1,000 policies a year, about 32% of all current site clicks |
| Medicinal cannabis | Named TGA 2026-27 priority focus area ("Medicinal cannabis"), alongside "Weight loss medications" and "Erectile dysfunction medications" |
| US fork (referlabs.com) | Self-serve rates are US$5 to US$80 per lead; even Katalys's US$175 default needs about 9,000 US sessions a month, 34 times today's AU clicks, from a new domain (realistically two years or more) |
| Meal kits, fresh pet food, alcohol, fitness apps | No published payout reaches A$100 (best found: Marley Spoon A$30 a sale; Kic A$20) |
| Energy and telco "native referral codes" | Customer refer-a-friend schemes pay credit, not cash, and several ban posting on coupon sites (Amber names coupon websites) |
| Vush, KIC, RespectHealth (the code queries already reaching /deals) | Payout below A$100 (Vush at most A$40.77 an item; KIC A$20) or no current program |

Two corrections to the brief, both checked 19 Sep 2026: no live page states Moshy or Juniper payout rates, so there is nothing stale to fix; and the TGA list does not name "affiliate arrangements" or "AI-generated content" as focus areas (AI appears only as "AI-generated misinformation and deceptive endorsements" in the principles).

## 4. Sequence: one workstream at a time

**Workstream 1, 22 Sep to 31 Oct 2026: defend the telehealth code base.** All messages, no pages.
- Hims: ask whether referlabs.com.au and `/deals` are Approved Channels; get the answer in writing.
- Mosh: ask to match 58% on REFERAL55 (or retire PILOTPMG publicly), and to list REFERAL55 as a partner code on its terms page, as it already does with PAT50.
- Moshy: confirm REFERRAL120 is live, exclusive, and still paid on typed use; request monthly redemptions split by link versus typed code.
- PetsOnMe: get a tracked link (every conversion is currently untracked).
- **Kill gate, 31 Oct:** if Hims has not approved referlabs.com.au in writing, Juniper and Hims stay link-only with no code on the site. If Mosh will not reach value parity, show the merchants' own public codes, dated, beside ours (after checking the partner terms allow it) rather than let coupon sites own the "58%" query.

**Workstream 2, 1 Nov to 31 Dec 2026: the H1 test on a non-health page.** Get an EcoFlow publisher code; put it on `/ecoflow`, `/ecoflow-vs-anker-solix` and `/portable-power-station-australia`; supply the GA4 property ID so outbound clicks are counted per merchant.
- **Kill gate, 15 Nov:** no code issued means stop and record EcoFlow as plain.
- **Kill gate, 31 Dec:** compare EcoFlow (code) with Anker SOLIX (plain) on the same pages. No attributable EcoFlow sale means no evidence that codes carry outside telehealth, and workstream 3 does not start.

**Workstream 3, 5 Jan to 28 Feb 2027: one big-ticket code vertical,** only if workstream 2 passed. Pick one of Star RV or Lekker Bikes (not both), after confirming the price-comparison exclusion does not cover editorial pages. One hub, one brand page, the code dated per the onboarding standard.
- **Kill gate, 28 Feb:** no page in the cluster at an average position of 20 or better means stop; the vertical will not earn within the venture window at current authority.

## 5. Three unresolved questions

1. **Is referlabs.com.au an Approved Channel under the signed Hims agreement?** Settled by the agreement itself, or the Approved Channels list in the Hims Partner Dashboard, confirmed in writing by Hims. This decides whether candidate 2 exists at all.
2. **Does a code lift conversion outside telehealth (H1)?** Settled by workstream 2: an EcoFlow code against Anker SOLIX on the same pages for 60 days, with GA4 outbound clicks and Commission Factory conversions. Nothing in the site's history can answer it, because codes and exposure have always moved together.
3. **Where do the unattributed REFERRAL120 redemptions come from, and is the code exclusively ours?** Settled by Moshy's redemption timestamps split by link and typed code, a live checkout test, and Moshy confirming whether REFERRAL120 existed before 7 Jul 2026 (a cached third-party page pairs it with a "June 2025" title). The answer tells you whether search snippets and AI answers, which cost nothing to extend, are the real distribution channel.
