## ASSET 2 — HARO / SourceBottle response template

**Where:** SourceBottle (sourcebottle.com — AU-focused, free), Qwoted, and Featured.com.
Set a daily alert for categories: *Business & Finance, Health & Wellbeing, Technology,
Small Business.* Respond within a few hours — speed wins.

**Template (fill the brackets, keep it under 200 words):**

> Subject: [Restate their exact question in 6–8 words]
>
> Hi [Journalist name],
>
> Responding to your query on [topic]. I run Refer Labs, an independent Australian
> comparison platform, so I look at [category] across providers every week.
>
> **[Your 2–3 sentence answer — lead with the single most useful, specific insight.
> Give a concrete number or a clear "most people get X wrong" angle. No fluff.]**
>
> One thing readers often miss: [a genuinely useful, non-obvious point].
>
> Happy to expand or provide data if useful. You're welcome to attribute this to
> "Jarred, founder of Refer Labs (referlabs.com.au)".
>
> Thanks,
> Jarred — referlabs.com.au — jarred@referlabs.com.au

**Rules that get you picked:**
- Answer the exact question first; don't pivot to a pitch.
- Be specific and quotable — journalists lift sentences verbatim.
- Offer attribution with the URL, but never demand a link.
- 3–5 quality responses/week is plenty.

## ASSET 3 — Cold pitch / feature-swap email

**Who to send to:** AU newsletters, small blogs and creators in your verticals (weight-loss/
health, small-business/SaaS, side-income/finance) who'd find a specific comparison page
genuinely useful for their audience. Aim for 5–10 personalised sends/week.

**Template A — "useful resource" (for content owners):**

> Subject: A resource for your [topic] readers
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Really liked your piece on [specific article — name it]. The bit about [detail] matched
> what we keep seeing.
>
> I run Refer Labs, an independent Australian comparison site. We just published
> [Page title] ([URL]) — it compares [what it compares] without any pay-to-rank, which
> felt relevant to that article.
>
> If it's useful to your readers, a mention would be genuinely appreciated — and if
> there's anything of yours I can point our readers to in return, tell me and I'll take a
> look. Either way, keep up the good work.
>
> Cheers,
> Jarred — referlabs.com.au

**Template B — "get featured" (for complementary businesses/tools you review):**

> Subject: Featured you on Refer Labs
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Quick heads-up — we included [their tool] in our independent comparison here: [URL].
> It's a genuine, un-paid assessment (we never sell rankings).
>
> If you think it's fair, a link back from your "as featured in" / press page or a share
> would help both of us. And if you run a newsletter or blog, happy to explore a swap.
>
> Cheers,
> Jarred — referlabs.com.au

**Best pages to pitch (match to the recipient's audience):**
- Health/weight-loss → `/weight-loss`, `/best-weight-loss-telehealth-australia`
- Small business / SaaS → `/business-software`, `/best-ai-sales-tools`, `/nutshell`
- Creators → `/best-newsletter-platform`, `/best-website-builder`
- Side income / finance → `/polymarket`, `/incomelab`

**Cold-email rules:** one specific compliment (prove you read their work), one clear ask,
one line offering value back. Never attach anything. Follow up once after 5 days, then stop.

## Weekly cadence (30 min/day)
- **Mon:** 3 directory submissions + 5 cold pitches.
- **Tue–Fri:** answer SourceBottle/Featured queries as they land (speed matters).
- **Fri:** log what landed in a simple sheet (site, page linked, date). Repeat what works.

Target: **10+ referring domains in 30 days.** That's the signal that most accelerates
everything the SEO build already put in place.
