# Professional Services Vertical Strategy

**Status:** 🎯 Vertical-First Go-to-Market
**Target:** Law Firms, Accounting Firms, Consulting, Architecture, Engineering
**Date:** January 12, 2026

---

## Why Professional Services First?

### Market Characteristics
1. **High LTV:** $10K-$500K+ per client
2. **Relationship-driven:** 80% of new business comes from referrals already
3. **Compliance-sensitive:** Need audit trails, payout tracking
4. **Network effects:** Partners naturally refer to each other
5. **Underserved:** Current referral tools built for e-commerce

### Competitive Advantage
- **Trust-based sales:** Professional services clients need warm introductions
- **Long sales cycles:** Referral programs keep prospects engaged
- **Partner ecosystems:** Law firms refer accounting firms, etc.
- **Regulatory compliance:** Our ledger system is audit-ready

   - State bar compliant fee sharing

   - Refer overflow cases to trusted partners
   - Track referring attorney compensation

   - Track implementation partner referrals

## Pricing Strategy (Professional Services)

### Before (Generic SaaS)
- Base: $49/mo
- Scale: $199/mo
- Enterprise: Custom

### After (Value-Based for Pro Services)
- **Starter** ($99/mo): Solo practitioners, small firms (1-5 people)
  - Up to 50 active partners
  - Basic compliance tracking
  - Email/SMS campaigns

- **Professional** ($299/mo): Growing practices (5-20 people)
  - Up to 200 active partners
  - AI partner scoring
  - Advanced compliance ledger
  - Multi-user access

- **Firm** ($799/mo): Established firms (20+ people)
  - Unlimited partners
  - White-label portal
  - API access
  - Dedicated success manager

**Pricing Rationale:**
- Higher price reflects higher client LTV ($10K+ vs. $100)
- Positions as "professional tool" not "discount platform"
- Aligns with other pro services software ($200-1000/mo)

## Sales Process (Consultative, Not Self-Serve)

### Before (Product-Led Growth)
1. Sign up free
2. Import contacts
3. Send first campaign
4. Upgrade when ready

### After (Sales-Led for Enterprise)
1. **Discovery Call** (30 min)
   - Understand current referral process
   - Identify pain points (compliance, tracking, payouts)
   - Qualify: Do they have 20+ potential partners?

2. **Demo** (45 min)
   - Show vertical-specific features
   - Walk through compliance workflow
   - Present ROI projection

3. **Proposal** (custom)
   - Pricing based on firm size
   - Implementation timeline
   - Success metrics

4. **Onboarding** (2-4 weeks)
   - Import existing partner network
   - Configure compliance settings
   - Train team on platform

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Regulatory Complexity
**Mitigation:**
- Partner with legal compliance experts
- State-by-state templates
- Disclaimers and attorney review prompts

### Risk 2: Sales Cycle Length
**Mitigation:**
- Start with smaller firms (faster decisions)
- Offer pilot programs (30-day trials)
- Focus on quick wins (first referral in 7 days)

### Risk 3: Market Education
**Mitigation:**
- Content marketing (educate on benefits)
- Free tools (ROI calculators, templates)
- Speaking at industry events
